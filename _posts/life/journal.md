# Week 47 of the Year of our Lord 2024

## Sunday Nov 24, 2024 -- San Francisco, CA, USA

Maybe this day will be better? He thought as he fastened his shoes (the non wet ones) to take the morning walk. He went further and further to a place where you can see the city, the golden gate bridge, alcatraz, and parts of oakland. There were people fishing, casting their lines far into the waters of the bay. There were kayakers pushing through the torrents with disciplined pulses of energy. Dozens of ships were docked at the harbor, and people were working on repairing the boardwalks. The rains of the previous days gave way to warm rays of sunlight breaking through the clouds that christened the hills. When they fell on his skin, he felt that the day might turn to be good. On his way back, he decided to start getting some business in order. That seems to be the theme here, expecting a lot from the day, getting some back, going to bed with a frown. I would like to say that there was a lot of work done this way but that would be a lie. He tried. He really did. But, a person can only do so much before their own mind comes to the rescue and ruins the day. There was no way to know what was going on inside but there was a lot of sleeping, lot of starting into the abyss. A mix of hindi songs and darkness is a great recipe to rethink your life choices. One thing that a creature should avoid in times of despondence is comparing itself to other creatures of the same kind. Chris was doing the heavy lifting in the comparison department, looking at all sorts of people in software, music and arts and how their lives were objectively better than his. I have to give it to this guy, that sort of self awareness is a rare sight in todays self obsessed world. Yet, it really does no good when your life is quite shit. When the world is beating the shit out of you, you have to be your own best friend.

## Saturday Nov 23, 2024 -- San Francisco, CA, USA

No time for depression, he said in the morning waking up to go for the weekly run club that he forgot he signed up for. The shoes were still wet from the thundrous adventure yesterday. And the room did not dry them out because of the lack of ventilation. So now, he was stuck with having bought $10 worth of tickets for the movie the run club was watching later, yet chatgpt was pleading him not to run in wet shoes. Having sided with the machine, he swallowed the pain with some breakfast and decided to dedicate the day to getting stuff done. And there was increased focused activity this day compared to close to one month that he has been in this place. It kind of hit him, he has been in the city for close to a month -- what did he do? Towards the end of the day, the focus sessions petered out to give rise to long bouts of thought. Wanderous thought too. Going to sleep early surely did not help the thoughts that kept brimming to the surface. He thought maybe sleeping might help quiet them down.

## Friday Nov 22, 2024 -- San Francisco, CA, USA

Decided to turn things around and woke up at 8 am this time. Went straight to the pier to watch the ocean waves crash against the rocks. But the weather was terrible so his shoes were filled with water. The socks soaked everything up and it was possibly one of the worst feelings out there. He looked disgusted. But the expensive coffee in his hand with the overpized croissant was worth the pain. Battling the winds, we marched back to the hostel with his umbrella taking the brunt of his frustrations. He kept thinking to himself, how do people stay productive in this weather. Making a beeline for the bed, he rested. But there was a talk about Google Ads at Founders.Inc which he wanted to attend so he fought against the despair and made his way there. But after a while, it got boring so he drove the car in the pouring rain when there was clearly a flood warning on the weather app. All this was done to acquire a mcdonalds spicy sandwich. Not going to lie, I respect the hustle. After getting distracted a lot of once again pretending to get things done, he took a early nightcap. All this sleeping cannot be good for you right, is this person depressed once again?
